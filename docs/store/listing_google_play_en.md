## Full description (≤4000 chars)
DictaPro is a next-generation voice recorder. It doesn't just capture sound — it turns speech into text while you speak. All transcription runs right on your phone: no internet required, no files sent to third-party servers, no subscription needed for core features.

WHO IT'S FOR
— Doctors, lawyers, journalists: record a consultation → ready text in minutes
— Students: lectures become timestamped notes
— Business: meeting minutes and negotiation protocols without a stenographer
— Anyone dictating notes, letters and ideas on the go

HIGHLIGHTS
• On-device offline transcription. The accurate GigaAM model runs locally — your files never leave the phone
• Background recording with the screen off
• Long recordings: hours of audio are no problem; an interrupted transcription resumes where it stopped
• Full-text search across all recordings — find any moment by a word
• Export to TXT, HTML and PDF with timecodes
• Per-recording vocabulary: names and abbreviations recognized more accurately
• Light and dark themes

PRIVACY
Recordings, texts and keys stay on your device. Online transcription (optional) goes through our secured server only with your explicit consent.

«AI SUMMARIES» SUBSCRIPTION
Core features are free and ad-free. The subscription unlocks AI summaries of your recordings: key points, decisions, briefs — computed on our server and billed in AI-hours (1 hour = 1 hour of audio).

Requirements: Android 8.0+, 300 MB free space (recognition model downloaded once).
