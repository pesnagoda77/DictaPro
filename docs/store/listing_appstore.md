EN:
DictaPro is a voice recorder that turns speech into text by itself. The recognition model runs right on your iPhone — no internet needed, no recordings sent to third-party servers.

— Background recording, even with the screen off
— Offline Russian transcription (GigaAM model)
— Long recordings: interrupted transcription resumes where it stopped
— Full-text search across all recordings
— Export to TXT, HTML, PDF with timecodes
— Per-recording vocabulary: names and abbreviations recognized better
— Light and dark themes, native iOS feel

Privacy: recordings and texts stay on your device. Online AI summaries (subscription) run on our secured server, only with your consent.
